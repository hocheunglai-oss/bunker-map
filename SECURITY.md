# FCUNO / SPC Security Reporting

Status: Draft for Group Information Security validation.

Do not open a public repository issue for a suspected vulnerability. Report it
privately to `info@cosulich.it` with a subject that identifies FCUNO or SPC.

Include only the information needed to reproduce and assess the issue:

- the affected hostname, page or API route;
- a concise description and potential impact;
- safe reproduction steps and the time of observation;
- a reference to supporting evidence that can be exchanged securely; and
- contact details for follow-up, if appropriate.

Do not include passwords, session tokens, private keys, personal data,
commercial records or other secrets in ordinary email or repository content.
Group Information Security should confirm the approved secure-transfer method
when sensitive evidence is necessary.

## Handling states

Security reports may be tracked as `Received`, `Under Review`, `Mitigated` or
`Closed`. A technical change or test result does not by itself close a finding.
Only Group Information Security, the appointed independent reviewer or another
formally authorised risk owner may approve closure or risk acceptance.

This draft does not promise a response time, remediation deadline, safe-harbour
term, disclosure date or bounty. Those matters require Group Information
Security and, where relevant, Legal approval.

Internal handling and evidence steps are recorded in
[`docs/spc-security-operations-runbook.md`](docs/spc-security-operations-runbook.md).
