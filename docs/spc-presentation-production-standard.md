# SPC Presentation Production Standard

This standard applies to every section of **Incorporate AI into Trading (Intermediate)**.

## Audience And Language

- Write for bunker traders and management, not software specialists.
- Use short, direct sentences and familiar trading terms.
- Explain technical ideas in plain English before naming the technology.
- Use only words that advance the point. Avoid filler and promotional language.
- Prefer a calm boardroom tone. The audience includes non-native English speakers.
- Use **challenge**, not **problem**, throughout the presentation.

## Narration

- Use ElevenLabs `Anna - British Neutral Narration` for every chapter and section.
- Use `Eleven Multilingual v2` with speed `1.00`, stability `0.50`, similarity `0.75`, style exaggeration `0`, and speaker boost enabled.
- Generate one continuous take per chapter, then split only at section boundaries.
- Match the approved Introduction pace. Do not accelerate lists or later sections.
- Preserve the speaker's age, accent, pitch, tone, energy, and microphone distance.
- Begin the introduction with three seconds of silence.
- Generate lossless audio, master it once, and use only one final lossy encode.
- Verify the final MP4 transcript against the approved script before publishing.

## Visual Direction

- Build for 1920 x 1080 and verify at the final rendered resolution.
- Use one clear hierarchy: chapter opening, challenge opening, demonstration, summary.
- Do not add small chapter or section labels in a corner.
- Keep each screen focused on one idea, with no more than four short points.
- Use realistic workflow demonstrations for operational actions.
- Use restrained motion, purposeful transitions, and the Fratelli Cosulich navy, red, and white palette.
- Avoid card-heavy slide layouts when a full-screen sequence can explain the idea more clearly.
- Keep typography, spacing, logo position, progress treatment, and transition timing consistent.

## Quality Gate

- Confirm all visible wording against the editor content.
- Confirm narration speed, voice identity, volume, and opening silence by measurement.
- Confirm every spoken word through final-video transcription.
- Review representative and transition frames for clipping, overlap, blank screens, and timing.
- Test each section and `PLAY CHAPTER` on the production site after publication.
